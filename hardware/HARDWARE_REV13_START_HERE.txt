CURRENT DESIGN
- Laptop USB powers the ESP32-S3 through its USB socket. Do not also feed its
  external 5 V / 3.3 V power pins from a buck or other supply.
- A separately reviewed AC source supplies the enclosed transformer primary.
- Proposed transformer: 230 V AC to 12-0-12 V AC, listed 1.5 A, 50-60 Hz.
  Verify the actual winding and VA ratings. The planner uses ONE 12 V section,
  not 24 V end-to-end. The centre tap is not protective earth.
- Three relays switch three parallel secondary-side resistor branches.
  At nominal 12 V RMS, 68 ohm branches draw 0.176 / 0.353 / 0.529 A and
  dissipate 2.12 / 4.24 / 6.35 W. The alternative 33 ohm planner gives
  0.364 / 0.727 / 1.091 A and 4.36 / 8.73 / 13.09 W.
  These are calculations, not measurements or transformer losses.
- Chassis resistors retain the 50 W capacity proposal with specified heatsink
  mounting and a guard. Load values, fusing and thermal cutoff need verification.
- Voltage sensors connect across the measured circuit; current sensors are in
  series. Primary and secondary sensing are separate, reviewed interfaces.
- ACS712 5 A is a secondary candidate; reconsider the small primary-current
  measurement. ZMPT101B needs calibration and signal-range checks at 12 V AC.
- Four electrical measurement inputs and three relay-control pins are pending.
  There is no new build-ready ADC/relay pin map. Signal conditioning must suit
  the ESP32 range; no raw mains divider or primary return connects to the MCU.
- PT100 A measures transformer EXTERIOR temperature; PT100 B measures ambient.
  Neither directly measures oil or winding hot-spot temperature.
- NFC, OLED and the established low-voltage SPI/I2C/control pin assignments
  remain in the connection table. Check the exact purchased boards.
- The proposed 12 V ~7 Ah battery and buck are unconfirmed and optional.
  A buck takes DC input, never direct AC. Relay/sensor auxiliary supplies and
  the separate 12 V DC fan branch remain to be selected and budgeted.

BUILD BOUNDARIES
This is a design handoff, not dimensionally accurate CAD or an electrically
tested circuit. A qualified lab technician must prepare the enclosed primary
section and review source, fusing, disconnect, clearances, strain relief,
thermal cutoff, sensor isolation and grounding before energising.
An inverter creates hazardous high voltage even from a 12 V battery.
Do not reverse-feed the transformer or connect to a distribution transformer.
Do not attempt intentional shorts or operate beyond verified component ratings.

Firmware, physical NFC integration, relay feedback and a live telemetry adapter
still need implementation. Fit a separate BENCH-001 thermal model; keep the
NR-104 distribution-transformer replay separate. Surface readings and bench
results do not validate field winding hot-spot, oil temperature or remaining life.
Real watts require valid time-aligned AC voltage/current processing; RMS voltage
times RMS current gives apparent power. Record actual heating response; do not
promise an instant temperature change during a short demonstration.
