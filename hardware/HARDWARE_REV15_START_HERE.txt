CHOSEN BUILD
Existing college 230 V / 50 Hz AC supply -> technician-prepared enclosed
transformer -> one verified nominal 12 V AC secondary section -> three
relay-switched parallel resistor branches.

Borrow an enclosed college transformer trainer first. If unavailable, have the
lab technician prepare and check the proposed 230 V to 12-0-12 V AC transformer
assembly. Confirm output rating and permission to use it at the venue.
The source block in the 3D view represents the protected supply connection;
it is NOT another AC converter to buy. Geometry is illustrative, not exact CAD.

Removed from the active model and connection table:
- Inverter, battery and battery-fed buck branch.
- Primary hobby voltage/current sensor modules; primary sensing is deferred.

POWER AND SENSING
- Laptop USB port -> ESP32-S3 USB socket for power and serial data.
  Do not add an external 5 V / 3.3 V feed to the USB-powered controller.
- Separate enclosed regulated DC supply -> compatible relay/sensor modules.
  Verify its current budget, voltage, polarity, noise and signal grounding /
  isolation before connecting. A USB cable does not isolate the laptop.
- 12 V AC IS NOT 12 V DC. Do not feed the secondary into the ESP32, relay
  power pins, an ordinary DC buck converter.
- Core electrical sensing: one secondary ACS712 5 A candidate and one
  secondary ZMPT101B candidate. Calibrate at the actual current and 12 V AC.
  Voltage sensing is across the winding section; current sensing is in series
  with the load feed. The functional routes are not terminal-level wiring.
- TWO analog inputs need module-specific ESP32-compatible conditioning,
  filtering, protection and pin allocation. Do not directly connect a 5 V
  module output or raw AC conductor to a GPIO.
- Three relay command pins/drivers remain pending. Verify logic polarity,
  3.3 V compatibility, coil supply and default-off states. No GPIO drives a coil.
- PT100 A + MAX31865 measures transformer EXTERIOR; PT100 B measures ambient.
  Neither reading is oil temperature or winding hot-spot temperature.
- Room temperature stays because the problem statement includes ambient
  conditions. Surface minus room temperature gives temperature rise.
  Use a small supported probe in room air; no large stand is required.
- The laptop is the operator interface. Accessory fixtures and their wiring
  have been removed from the active build. No additional display or panel is
  needed. Keep the protection and enclosures; they are part of the core build.

LOAD PLAN
The calculations use ONE 12 V RMS winding section, not 24 V end-to-end.
Confirm the actual transformer's winding/current/VA rating and terminals.
The centre tap is not protective earth. Do not reverse-feed the transformer.

68 ohm branches: 1 / 2 / 3 on = 0.176 / 0.353 / 0.529 A RMS;
resistive load power = 2.12 / 4.24 / 6.35 W at exactly 12 V RMS.
Alternative 33 ohm candidate: 0.364 / 0.727 / 1.091 A RMS;
4.36 / 8.73 / 13.09 W. Verify actual component ratings before use.
The 50 W chassis resistor rating is capacity under specified mounting/cooling,
not actual consumption. Use the required heatsink/guard and thermal protection.
The planner toggles do not operate real relays.

MEASUREMENT CLAIMS
Secondary RMS voltage/current, loading and surface/ambient temperature can
support a live monitoring demonstration after acquisition is implemented.
Vrms * Irms is apparent power in VA. Output real watts require time-aligned
waveforms and phase calibration. Do not claim measured transformer efficiency
or input losses without input and output real-power measurements.
Fit a separate BENCH-001 thermal model. Keep NR-104 distribution-transformer
replay separate; this bench does not validate field oil/winding temperatures,
fault diagnosis or remaining useful life. Record actual heating time and use
clearly labelled replay if thermal changes are too slow for a short demo.

WHAT STILL NEEDS WORK
Qualified lab technician: protected mains assembly, enclosure, disconnect,
fuses, cable retention, earthing/isolation, temperature-probe mounting and
independent thermal protection. Keep mains terminals inaccessible. Do not
intentionally short or exceed verified ratings; never connect to an energised
distribution transformer.
Hardware team: exact modules, two analog channels, three relay drivers/pins,
DC supply/current budget, calibrated sampling and commissioning.
Software team: ESP32 firmware, live ingestion adapter and load-response
confirmation are still unimplemented. The viewer is a design handoff.
