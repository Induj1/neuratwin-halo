Hardware guide ↗
Transformer identification is unresolved. A–B ≈7 Ω; paths involving C ≈200–300 Ω. No software setting establishes electrical safety or authorises applying 230 V. Record an actual technician review before clearing holds.

Outstanding holds

Physical K4 switching stays locked. Software is not an independent current or temperature protection device.

Optional room-temperature reference

For surface-model fitting when there is no ambient probe, record an actual independent room measurement. It expires after 10 minutes. Do not enter a guessed constant.

Sensor calibration

Record reference-based scale factors after safe ADC conditioning. Values are per millivolt of the ADC waveform RMS, after DC-bias removal.
