Power domain / conditional functional diagram

Load path after the 12 V pair is verified

All connections below use functional labels, not assumed physical screw positions. Filled dots are connected junctions. Contacts are drawn de-energised (COM–NC); NO is open. Secondary return is separate from ESP32/relay logic ground.

Per branch: AC feed → relay COM → closed NO contact → resistor → common secondary return. All four NC contacts are unused. Use an insulated, mechanically supported common-return solder junction or suitable connector; no exposed twisted joints. Keep hot resistors away from wiring and touch access.

Physical board contact order reported by your multimeter: LEFT = NO · MIDDLE = COM · RIGHT = NC, in the same viewing orientation used during that test. Reconfirm COM–NC continuity off and COM–NO continuity on before wiring. This is specific to your board.

Transformer identification

A/B/C and D/E remain unassigned.

Three-wire group: A = blue, B = red, C = blue. Separate pair D/E = red/red. Reported DC resistance: A–B ≈ 7 Ω; B–C and A–C ≈ 200–300 Ω; D–E ≈ 441 Ω; groups mutually OL. This asymmetry prevents a reliable center-tap assignment. Colour and physical placement do not establish voltage rating.

Required evidence: clear manufacturer nameplate/datasheet with input voltage/frequency, winding lead map, secondary rating and isolation information; repeatable disconnected, clipped resistance readings. DMM OL is not an insulation withstand test. A competent lab technician must determine suitable further tests.

Load budget / arithmetic only

Three branches are already close to 1 A.

At 12 V and 39 Ω: 0.308 A and 3.69 W per branch; three branches = 0.923 A; four = 1.231 A.

At the measured 37.8 Ω, three branches draw about 0.952 A at 12 V. Output above 12 V increases current and heating. The 10 W marking does not make the resistor cool or establish its permitted mounting conditions. This calculator does not approve loading or override independent protection.

Commissioning evidence / before the demonstration

Hardware acceptance is still outstanding.

  1. Resolve transformer winding identity and exact controller family; record reviewer and supporting evidence.
  2. Lab technician verifies enclosure, isolation, grounding, switching, fuse and independent overcurrent/thermal protection.
  3. Confirm relay off/reset states, contact map and resistor mounting with power isolated. Keep K4 disabled.
  4. Verify every sensor interface and ADC peak range. Record calibration against independent voltage, current and temperature references.
  5. After approval, acquire sensor-only data using the explicit USB bridge. Opening a serial port may reset the board.
  6. Run one approved load branch, capture actual V/I/surface response, and compare independent readings with software readings.
  7. Export the physical session and record losses, gaps and unavailable channels. Software acknowledgment is not evidence of relay-contact operation.

The application’s industrial simulation works independently of this hold. Do not clear commissioning flags just for presentation.

What the physical demonstration establishes

It can establish: acquired electrical/surface measurements, their reference error, controllable low-voltage load response after commissioning, freshness monitoring, event capture and maintenance workflow.

It cannot establish: field transformer fault-prediction accuracy, oil/winding measurements, distribution-transformer remaining life, or mains safety certification.

Component references: Espressif ESP32 datasheet · Analog Devices DS18B20 documentation · HSE electrical safety guidance. These do not identify your unknown transformer or certify a third-party module.
