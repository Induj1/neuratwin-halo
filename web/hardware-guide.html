What is on the bench

Protected transformer, one voltage sensor, one current sensor, two temperature sensors, three relay/resistor load branches, ESP32, sensor interfaces and the necessary supplies/enclosures. The laptop supplies the display and operator controls.

Why keep the room-temperature sensor?

The problem statement includes ambient temperature. Comparing transformer surface temperature with room temperature gives temperature rise = surface − ambient, so a warmer room is not mistaken for extra transformer heating. It is a small sensor in room air, not another test fixture. Surface temperature remains a bench measurement, not oil or winding temperature.

    LOAD PLANNER / CALCULATED, NOT LIVE

    Three relays. Repeatable load steps.

    Nominal 12 V AC RMS across each resistor. Start with the existing 68 Ω / 50 W proposal, or compare a 33 Ω candidate. These toggles do not switch real hardware.

    OUTPUT CURRENT0.18 A RMS
    LOAD POWER2.1 W
    EQUIVALENT R68.0 Ω

    Each 68 Ω branch dissipates 2.12 W at 12 V RMS. Three branches: 0.529 A / 6.35 W. These are nominal resistive-load calculations, not measured transformer losses.

    Mount the resistors on a suitable guarded heatsink. The 50 W rating depends on mounting and cooling. Verify the winding’s current/VA rating and actual RMS voltage; do not reuse the old 24 V calculations.

    Can we use a rheostat instead?

    A properly rated laboratory rheostat can work after its Ω, current and power limits are checked. Fixed switched resistors are the proposed build because their steps are repeatable. A small signal potentiometer cannot carry this load. Prevent adjustment toward zero resistance.

    BUILD SCOPE

    Small AC bench. Separate field model.

    BENCH-001: a small dry transformer with staged resistive loads, reviewed AC sensing and exterior/ambient temperature probes. Fit its thermal behaviour independently. Secondary V / I can show loading and voltage response; input power and transformer efficiency are not measured in the core build.

    NR-104: distribution-transformer replay. This bench does not validate oil temperature, winding hot-spot, field fault diagnosis or remaining life.

    Live firmware, load-response confirmation and the ingestion adapter remain to be built. Transformer heating may be slow; record a measured run and keep replay visibly labelled.
