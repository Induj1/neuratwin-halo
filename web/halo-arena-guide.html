NEURATWIN HALO / NEURA RANGERS

Let the judge challenge
the decision.

All Arena data is synthetic. The physical bench, its commissioning holds and relay controls are separate. Arena requires the running local server; this presenter guide works offline.

1. Give the judge a hidden disturbance

Open Grid Rescue Arena. Create a blind investigation and open its separate judge screen. On localhost, use this laptop; the QR does not make the server reachable from a phone.

  1. For a quick demonstration, let the judge choose temperature sensor bias, 80%, start minute 5, duration 20 minutes. For a truly blind test, do not watch their selection.
  2. Return to the investigator and advance five minutes. The oil deviation supports more than one explanation.
  3. Request the independent oil probe. Explain why disagreement supports a channel problem. In a mixed cooling-and-bias scenario, one bad sensor does not explain all heating.
  4. Freeze the assessment, then reveal ground truth. Show the unchanged evidence fingerprint. Explain that alert timing does not measure diagnostic accuracy across a population.

Cooling obstruction is slower: try start 1, duration 60, severity 100%, then observe 50 minutes. A short run may correctly remain unresolved. Never promise immediate detection of every fault.

2. Show three futures

Use the default profile and compare. All branches begin at the same oil temperature and winding gradient. Scrub to minute 30: the unmitigated branch, delayed operator choice and immediate qualifying reduction separate visibly.

Explain the search result: smallest immediate reduction in 5% increments passing the declared load, oil and hot-spot bounds. The three-model envelope is sensitivity, not a confidence interval. The model may return no qualifying choice.

“Use latest case observation” copies the synthetic oil channel as observed, including any bias; it is not automatic model calibration. The winding gradient stays explicitly assumed.

3. Make capacity depend on time

Open Thermal capacity. The default load peak violates constraints, so extra capacity is zero. Set load at minutes 15 and 30 to 1.1 pu, then calculate again. Compare the 15-, 30- and 60-minute headroom. Raise ambient or starting oil temperature and recalculate to show the headroom shrink.

This is conditional extra load under declared assumptions; it does not increase the real transformer’s rating. Boundary results are rounded and have no independently established operational safety reserve.

4. Protect a neighbourhood

Set flexible demand multiplier to 2.0, leave outage at None, and search. Compare Before and With plan. Discuss served demand, curtailment, losses, each feeder’s voltage/current and the fixed 25 kW clinic load.

Disable transformer A and search again. The clinic has no modeled alternative supply, so the system must say No feasible plan. It cannot invent a tie or pretend another feeder restored the clinic.

The workshop may transfer only through a declared alternative supply. This is a balanced steady-state study, not a real switching or protection plan.

5. Give them the evidence

Download the Arena case: observations, requested checks, frozen assessment, revealed scenario if permitted, attached studies, readable HTML report and SHA-256 manifest. Each standalone study also has an export.
